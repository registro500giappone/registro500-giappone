Electrical Trouble Field Notes

You spread out the wiring diagram and still don't know where to start. — That was me. So I took one symptom at a time and drew, case by case, on a single diagram where the current goes and where it stops. I hope it helps when electrical trouble gets you lost.

Contents

    ⚠️ What's drawn is the wiring as it left the factory. Even the newest 500 is over fifty years old, and its wiring has often been worked on since. Extra fuses, added relays, aftermarket accessories, rewiring during repairs — all of it is common. If the diagram and your car disagree, the wiring in front of you is right.

    These notes are about the FIAT 500 (D, F, L, R). The wiring is drawn from the original 500 sources (the wiring diagrams in the factory owner's handbooks).

    Every lamp state and flow of current in the diagrams is a calculation result; no numbers were made to fit the pictures. Each part ends with a list of those checks.
