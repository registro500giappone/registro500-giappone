About this diagram

This is the wiring diagram of the Polish-built FIAT 126p ELX (1996–2000). It was redrawn by this site, tracing every wire of an ELX wiring diagram circulating in Poland. The parts are not placed where they sit on the real car; they are grouped into three areas: front, cabin and engine bay.

Some part names are inferred

The original diagram has no legend (list of part names). Its part numbers largely follow the earlier FL diagram, so the names were assigned from the FL legend, the ELX owner's manual (fuse table and warning lamps), the shape of each part and how it is wired. The names were then checked against descriptions of these parts in Polish workshop-manual extracts and owners' write-ups, and confirmed where they match the wiring (the control unit that takes engine speed from the ignition coil and a closed-throttle signal from the throttle switch and cuts the fuel with the solenoid, the choke warning lamp and buzzer, the crank sensor feeding the ignition module, the diagnostic connector with only two pins fitted). Only the names that could not be confirmed carry a * (headlamp relay, vacuum switch). The connections themselves follow the labels of the original.

How to read it

  • The line colour is the colour of the wire's insulation. Two-colour wires are drawn in the main colour with a dashed stripe in the second colour.
  • Points earthed to the body are shown with an earth symbol next to the part.
  • The numbered long boxes are connectors joining sections of the harness. 25–27 are the steering column switches, 28 the ignition switch, and 40–43 the joints between the front and rear harnesses. The numbers are the part numbers of the original.
  • Fuse numbers and ratings follow the table in the owner's manual (12 fuses, no. 3 unused). Fuses 5 and 6, 8 and 9, and 11 and 12 share their supply side.
  • The small black dots (JOINT) are where wires branch inside the harness.
  • The thick cable from the battery to the starter is drawn in grey, because the original gives no colour for it.

Main specifications (126p ELX, from the owner's manual)

  • Electrical system: 12 V, negative earth / battery 32 Ah
  • Charging: alternator (34 A, built-in regulator)
  • Starter: key operated (0.6 kW)
  • Ignition: breakerless electronic ignition (ignition module and crank sensor). There is no distributor; the coil feeds the two plugs directly
  • The charging, ignition and starting circuits have no fuses
  • Wire colours (codes in the original): b = white / c = black / f = violet / k = red / l = light blue / n = dark blue / o = brown / p = orange / r = pink / s = grey / t = green / z = yellow

Main differences from the FL (1985–94)

  • The fuses increase from 8 to 12; horn/hazard, interior lamp/lighter, heated rear window and rear fog each get their own fuse
  • The headlamps are switched by a relay (inferred)
  • The turn signal and hazard flashers are combined into one unit
  • Ignition becomes electronic (no distributor, condenser or ballast resistor wire)
  • The idle cut-off solenoid is driven by a control unit instead of its own fuse

Wiring may differ by year and specification. Always check against the actual car before working on it.
