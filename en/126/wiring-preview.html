About this diagram

This is the wiring diagram of the FIAT 126 early model (1972–76). It was redrawn by this site, tracing every wire of the overall wiring diagram in the English-language workshop manual (Autobook, Fiat 126 1972-76). The parts are not placed where they sit on the real car; they are grouped into three areas: front, cabin and engine bay.

How to read it

  • The line colour is the colour of the wire's insulation. Two-colour wires are drawn in the main colour with a black or white dashed stripe.
  • Points earthed to the body are shown with an earth symbol next to the part. The white-black (BIANCO-NERO) wire is earthed at the flasher unit's mounting; the earths of the right side repeater, the wiper switch and the horn button run through it.
  • "JUNCTION" is a connector joining sections of the harness. In the original both are at the boundary between the front and the cabin (one on the right, one on the left). Here the left one is drawn below the cabin to keep the lines readable.
  • The fuse box letters A–H are as printed in the original diagram.

Main specifications (early model)

  • Electrical system: 12 V, negative earth
  • Charging: dynamo (not an alternator)
  • Starter: cable (lever) operated — the key does not crank the engine
  • Instruments: with fuel gauge
  • Fuses: 8
  • Wire colours: Azzurro = light blue / Bianco = white / Blu = dark blue / Giallo = yellow / Grigio = grey / Marrone = brown / Nero = black / Rosso = red / Verde = green / Viola = violet

Differences from the 500

  • A fuel gauge (sender in the tank plus gauge in the instrument panel)
  • 8 fuses (high and low beams on separate fuses for each side)
  • Side repeaters on the front wings
  • The tail lamps are fed crosswise (the fuse of the left front side light feeds the right tail lamp, and vice versa)

Details vary with year, market and modifications (later models add a heated rear window, for example). Fuse ratings are reference values taken from the table in a later manual. Always check against the actual car before working on it.
